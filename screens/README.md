# Screen designs for the scroll

`template.png` (and `template.svg`) is one screen of the paper scroll at its exact print size:
**576 × 800 px = 72 × 100 mm** (the printer prints 8 px per mm).

- **White area = what shows through the cassette's window: 504 × 720 px (63 × 90 mm),**
  starting 72 px from the left and 40 px from the top.
- **Grey = hidden** behind the window frame. Fine to draw into (it just won't show), except:
- **The black bar on the left is the registration mark** the machine steers by. The machine
  adds it itself, so leave that spot white in your design.
- **Black and white only.** Greys get printed as dot patterns.
- Screen 0 is START, the last one is the top/win screen.

Save each design as a PNG (576 × 800), named by its place on the scroll: `00.png` (START), `01.png`, `02.png` …
